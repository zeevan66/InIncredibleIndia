India e-Visa Information

⚠️ Important: Apply at least 4 days before your intended travel date. e-Visas are available for 169 countries.

What is India e-Visa?

India e-Visa is an electronic travel authorization that allows eligible foreign nationals to enter India for tourism, business, or medical purposes. The entire application process is online.

Step-by-Step Application Process

Step 1: Prepare Documents
  • Passport (valid for 6 months from arrival date)
  • Recent passport-size photograph
  • Scanned copy of passport bio page
  • Payment method (credit/debit card)
Step 2: Fill Online Application
  • Visit official Indian visa portal
  • Complete application form
  • Upload documents
  • Double-check all information
Step 3: Pay Visa Fee
  • Fee varies by nationality ($25-$100)
  • Pay online via card
  • Save payment receipt
Step 4: Receive e-Visa
  • Processing time: 3-5 days
  • e-Visa sent via email
  • Print copy for travel
  • Present at immigration

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to get an e-Visa?
Typically 3-5 business days. We recommend applying at least 4 days before travel, but you can apply up to 120 days in advance.
Can I extend my e-Visa?
e-Tourist Visas cannot be extended. You must exit India before the visa expires.
What if my e-Visa application is rejected?
You can reapply after correcting the errors. Alternatively, apply for a regular visa at an Indian embassy/consulate.
Do I need to print my e-Visa?
Yes, carry a printed copy of your e-Visa when traveling to India. Immigration officials may request it.
